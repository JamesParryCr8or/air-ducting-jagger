What We Do
COMPLETE VENTILATION
SOLUTIONS

End-to-end commercial kitchen extraction and ducting services — from initial survey and bespoke design through to full installation and ongoing maintenance.

🏗️

KITCHEN EXTRACTION SYSTEMS

Complete canopy, ductwork, and extract fan installation for commercial kitchens of all sizes. Designed for maximum grease capture and airflow efficiency.

🌀

DUCTING INSTALLATION

Bespoke galvanised and stainless steel ductwork — fabricated to fit your premises precisely. Spiral, rectangular, and custom configurations available.

💨

FRESH AIR SUPPLY

Make-up air and input ventilation systems to balance your extraction, maintain comfort, and ensure compliance with Building Regulations Part F.

⚡

GAS INTERLOCK SYSTEMS

Life-safety gas interlock installations linking your extraction system to gas supply — mandatory for all commercial gas appliances under BS 6173.

🔇

NOISE & ODOUR CONTROL

Acoustic attenuators, anti-vibration mounts, and carbon/ESP filtration to keep neighbours happy and meet planning conditions.

🔄

FAN REPLACEMENT & REPAIR

Emergency and planned extract fan replacements. We carry common motor sizes for same-day repair — minimising kitchen downtime.
